Lidl, Aldi, Dirk, Jumbo and Albert Heijn

Fetches the latest Lidl, Aldi, Dirk and Jumbo offers and this week's AH bonus. This runs by itself every morning at 06:00 and on Friday at 20:00 (for next week's offers); use the button for an extra run. New prices appear on the deals page after about three minutes.

Albert Heijn: next week

This week's AH offers come in by themselves. AH announces next week's bonus on Friday; to show it before Monday, upload it:

  1. Open ah.nl/bonus (for next week's offers, pick next week at the top of that page) and scroll to the bottom so every offer loads.
  2. Press Ctrl+S and save as "Webpage, Complete".
  3. Choose which week it is and the saved .html file, and upload it. Next week's offers appear under Next week and move into this week by themselves on Monday at 06:00.
